# Team Topologies

**Picture how your teams work together.** Draw your organisation with [Team Topologies](https://teamtopologies.com/): the four team types and three interaction modes from the book by Matthew Skelton and Manuel Pais, in the colours of the official shape templates. Picture how your teams work today, discuss changes, and keep the result as a plain file next to your code.

::: tip Try it now
Open the [web app](https://team-topologies.modeler.miragon.io): no install, no account.
:::

![The Team Topologies web app editing its example](/shots/team-topologies/demo.gif)

## Use it where you work

- **In the browser:** [team-topologies.modeler.miragon.io](https://team-topologies.modeler.miragon.io)
- **In VS Code:** the [Team Topologies Modeler](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=miragon-gmbh.team-topologies-modeler) opens your topologies as diagrams. Also on [Open VSX](https://open-vsx.org/extension/miragon-gmbh/team-topologies-modeler).
- **In Orca:** [open the plugin in Orca](https://www.onorca.dev/plugins/miragon/orca-modelers/team-topologies-modeler): each change your coding agent writes shows up as it happens ([what it does in Orca](https://github.com/Miragon/modeler-suite/tree/main/modelers/team-topologies/apps/orca#readme)).
- **In your own app:** `npm install @miragon/team-topologies-renderer`

## What you can draw

- Stream-aligned, platform, enabling and complicated-subsystem teams
- Collaboration, X-as-a-Service and facilitating
- A PNG export that opens again as a diagram

## File formats

`.tt` (text) and `.ttm.json`, plus editable `*.tt.png`.
