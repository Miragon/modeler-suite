## C4 model: the software architecture lens

Draw the **C4 model** of your software: the system landscape, the context of a
system, its containers and their components, as views of one model. Drill down
from a system to its containers and back up the breadcrumb.

Files use the `.c4` extension (and `.dsl`, such as `workspace.dsl`) and open in
the C4 Modeler. They hold a Structurizr DSL workspace, read and written
losslessly, so the Structurizr tools still open them.

**Open the C4 Modeler tutorial** (the button on the left) for a guided
"create your first diagram" walkthrough from the modeler itself.
