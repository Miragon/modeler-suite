## Business Model Canvas: the business model lens

Fill in the **Business Model Canvas** of one business model: nine blocks of
stickies, from the customer segments you serve to the costs it takes. Give a
sticky a tape colour that means what your team decides, and keep notes on what
you still have to test.

Files use the `.bmc` extension and open in the Business Model Canvas Modeler, as
plain YAML. The Business Model Canvas is by Strategyzer AG (strategyzer.com),
licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0.

**Open the Business Model Canvas tutorial** (the button on the left) for a
guided "create your first canvas" walkthrough from the modeler itself.
