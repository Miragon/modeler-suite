## Value Chains: the process landscape lens

Lay out the **value-adding steps** of a business as a chain of chevrons, break
them down into sub-steps and assign the **organizational units** responsible
for them, in the ARIS value-added chain notation.

Files use the `.vc` / `.vc.json` extension and open in the Value Chain Modeler,
with a deterministic JSON model.

**Open the Value Chain tutorial** (the button on the left) for a guided
"create your first value chain" walkthrough from the modeler itself.
