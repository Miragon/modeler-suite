# Open the example

The example is the team topology of an online shop: three stream-aligned teams, an enabling team,
a complicated-subsystem team and a platform team, connected by all three interaction modes, with a
flow of change and an annotation.

**Do this now:** click **Create diagram from example** in the step on the left. (You can also run
**Team Topologies: New Diagram from Example** from the Command Palette, or choose
**File › New File… › New Team Topologies Diagram from Example**.)

Choose where to save it; the diagram opens in the visual editor, pre-filled with teams and
interactions you can rearrange and learn from. Not sure which shape is which? Switch on the
**Legend** in the menu at the top left.
