# Open the example

Click [Create map from example](command:wardley.newMapFromExample) to save the example as a new
file. It maps a tea shop: its business and public customers want a cup of tea, which needs a cup,
tea and hot water, and the hot water needs water and a kettle that runs on power. The kettle is
about to evolve, and the power is outsourced.

You can also run **Wardley: New Map from Example** from the Command Palette, or choose
**File › New File… › New Wardley Map from Example**.

**Learn the basics** explains how to read the two axes of the map.
