# Learn the basics

- Drag a **sticky** from the palette into one of the nine blocks, or focus the entry and press
  Enter to add one in the middle. Stickies flow in file order; drop one between two others to put
  it there.
- Double-click a sticky (or press E) to edit its text. Alt with an arrow key moves the selected
  sticky up or down in its block, or into the block beside, above or below it.
- **Colour** in a sticky's context pad gives it one of eight tape colours, or none. What a colour
  means is your team's to decide, such as the customer segment a sticky serves.
- **Canvas title…** in the menu edits the title; a click on the title opens it too.
- **Notes** in the menu, or N, opens the notes panel. Write a note on the selected sticky, on a
  block or on the whole canvas; the comment at the end of each block heading and after the title
  opens it on that place.
- **Keyboard shortcuts…** in the menu at the top left lists every key.
