# Open the example

**Business Model Canvas: New Business Model Canvas from Example** creates the canvas of the Miragon
Modeler Suite: three customer segments, each with its own tape colour, the stickies that serve
one of them in that colour, and two notes, on a value proposition and on Cost Structure. You can
also choose it in the New File menu: **File › New File… › New Business Model Canvas from Example**.

Zoom with Cmd/Ctrl + and −, press Shift+1 to fit the canvas, and use the menu at the top left to
export it as PNG or SVG. Every export carries the attribution of the canvas.
